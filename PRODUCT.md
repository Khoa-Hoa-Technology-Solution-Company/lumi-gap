# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, students, reviewers, and research teams who need to search scientific literature, inspect trends, assess papers, prepare reports, and identify research gaps without losing time to fragmented tools.

## Product Purpose

PaperLens is an AI-assisted scientific publication analysis workspace. It combines academic metadata, semantic search, trend analysis, evidence-grounded reports, paper review, and collaborative research workflows in one dependable interface.

## Brand Personality

Serious, trustworthy, and data-driven. The product should feel calm, precise, and premium-academic while remaining practical for dense daily research work.

## Anti-references

Avoid image-forward magazine layouts, bright saturated palettes, gradient noise, decorative animation, low-information landing-page patterns, and unfamiliar controls that slow down expert workflows.

## Design Principles

- Respect researchers' time with clear hierarchy and direct navigation.
- Keep dense information readable instead of artificially sparse.
- Make evidence, system status, and AI-generated output easy to verify.
- Use familiar product patterns consistently across all workflows.
- Support focused reading in both bright and low-light environments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve visible keyboard focus, keep navigation order logical, label icon-only controls for screen readers, support reduced motion, and never communicate meaning through color alone.
